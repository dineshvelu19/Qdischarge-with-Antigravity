AI draft is generated within 60–90 seconds of discharge initiation. Navigate to Summary Review on the patient's discharge panel to access the draft.
✏️Physician Review Workflow
  • 1
    Open Summary Review. The draft appears section-by-section: Chief Complaint → Hospital Course → Investigations → Procedures → Discharge Medications → Follow-up Instructions → Condition at Discharge.
  • 2
    Every field is editable. Tap any section to enter edit mode. Override AI content wherever clinical judgment differs. All overrides are logged with your credentials and timestamp in the audit trail.
  • 3
    Review the ICD-10 / SNOMED CT Suggestion Panel in the right sidebar. Accept, modify, or reject each code suggestion. Accepted codes are embedded in the document and transmitted to billing for DRG/package mapping.
  • 4
    Verify the Medication Reconciliation Preview — side-by-side comparison of admission vs. discharge medications with change flags. Do not finalize until pharmacy confirms reconciliation (Phase 3 status indicator).
  • 5
    Tap Sign Discharge Summary. Confirm credentials (PIN or biometric). Digital signature applied with timestamp, user ID, and session metadata.
⚖️Clinical Accountability & Sign-Off Gate
⚠
The AI draft is a decision-support tool. Clinical accountability for the discharge summary rests entirely with the reviewing and signing physician. The AI module has no autonomous sign authority under any configuration of Qdischarge.
  • 1
    If any mandatory field is unpopulated, a Missing Fields Alert blocks sign-off and lists each incomplete item — enforcing NABH Std. COP.9 documentation completeness before finalization.
  • 2
    Post-sign amendments require a separate Addendum workflow. Original signed entries are never overwritten — amendments are clearly marked with a version chain in the audit trail.
  • 3
    The signed discharge summary constitutes a formal medical record under the Indian Evidence Act (electronic records provisions) and applicable medico-legal standards.
🔒
Once signed, the summary status changes to LOCKED — PHYSICIAN SIGNED. The document is transmitted to downstream departments and the patient portal simultaneously.

💊Pharmacy Task — Medication Reconciliation
  • 1
    Pharmacy receives the discharge medication list via FHIR MedicationRequest resource. Reconciliation workflow is assigned to the ward pharmacist automatically.
  • 2
    If pharmacy raises a Medication Conflict Flag (drug-drug interaction or formulary mismatch), you receive an in-app alert. Tap the flag to review and authorize or modify the order without leaving Qdischarge.
  • 3
    Pharmacist marks status MEDS READY when the discharge prescription pack is prepared and checked. Dashboard updates in real time.
🏥Nursing Task — Education & Clearance
  • 1
    Nursing receives: final vitals check assignment, IV removal and wound care task, and a patient education session task (verbal + written instruction materials).
  • 2

  • 3

💳Billing — Pre-Auth & Final Bill
  • 1
    Billing receives ICD-10 codes from the signed summary for DRG/package-based billing. Insurance pre-auth status is auto-checked against the payer database.
  • 2
    If an Authorization Hold is flagged, discharge can proceed if you tap Clinical Urgency Override with documented justification — logged immutably in the audit trail.
  • 3
    Billing marks BILLING CLEARED when the final bill is generated and co-pay/TPA status confirmed. Patient billing summary is queued for portal delivery.
🤝Care Coordination — Post-Discharge Plan
  • 1

  • 2

  • 3
    Coordinator marks CARE PLAN READY when all post-discharge arrangements are confirmed.

🛡️Pre-Discharge Completeness Validation
  • 1
    Before allowing finalization, Qdischarge runs a Documentation Completeness Check against the hospital-configured mandatory field list.
  • 2
    Mandatory fields enforced: Final diagnosis + ICD-10 code · Discharge medication list · Follow-up instructions · Condition at discharge · Physician sign-off · Nursing checklist co-sign.

⚖️Medico-Legal & Regulatory Alignment
⚠
Audit logs are stored in an append-only encrypted data store. No user — including system administrators — can modify or delete entries. This architecture satisfies requirements under the Indian Evidence Act (electronic records provisions) and Clinical Establishments Act documentation standards.
  • 1
    Access any patient's audit trail via Case History → Discharge Audit Log. Filter by user, action type, or time range.
  • 2
    Export audit trail as a signed PDF for regulatory submission, NABH internal audit, or legal proceedings — available to authorized users from the case record.
  • 3
    All data transmitted between Qdischarge, HIS/EHR, and downstream systems uses HL7 FHIR R4 with end-to-end TLS 1.3 encryption and OAuth 2.0 access control.
